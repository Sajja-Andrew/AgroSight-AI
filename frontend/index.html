Crop Categories

Common Crop Diseases

Explore diseases affecting different crop types and learn how to manage them

Legumes

Beans, peas, groundnuts, soybeans and more

Common Diseases:

  • Bean Rust: Reddish-brown pustules on leaves
  • Powdery Mildew: White powdery spots on leaves
  • Root Rot: Wilting and yellowing of plants
Apply fungicides, crop rotation, plant resistant varieties

Cash Crops

Coffee, tea, cotton, sugarcane, tobacco

Common Diseases:

  • Coffee Leaf Rust: Orange-yellow spots on leaves
  • Cotton Wilt: Sudden wilting and browning
  • Sugarcane Smut: Black whip-like growths
Remove infected plants, apply appropriate pesticides

Other Crops

Maize, rice, vegetables, fruits, cereals

Common Diseases:

  • Maize Lethal Necrosis: Yellowing and stunting
  • Rice Blast: Diamond-shaped lesions on leaves
  • Late Blight: Dark spots on leaves and fruits
Use certified seeds, proper spacing, balanced fertilization

Pest Management

Common Insect Pests

Identify and manage crop pests effectively

Fall Armyworm

Affects maize and other cereals. Look for leaf damage and larvae presence.

Control: Neem extract, early planting, biological control

Spider Mites

Tiny pests causing stippling on leaves. Common in dry conditions.

Control: Miticides, predatory mites, maintain humidity

Locusts

Swarming pests that devastate crops rapidly.

Control: Early warning systems, chemical control, biopesticides

Bean Beetles

Feed on leaves of legumes, causing skeletonization.

Control: Insecticidal soap, neem oil, row covers
